A few years ago I wrote {{linked_title "_posts/2021-12-11-reasons-why-my-website-is-offline.md"}}, in which I put on my tinfoil hat and pulled my whole website into the closet rather than let Copilot anywhere near my code. I was a principled curmudgeon about AI, and honestly? I stand by past me. He had reasons.

<!--more-->

But I've come around. Not because anyone argued me out of it, but because the thing turned out to be genuinely _delightful_ to talk to, and I am, at my core, a man who can be won over by delight.

Here's the context you need: I have been watching _Love Island_. I am a sucker for trash reality TV: the recouplings, the Casa Amor betrayals, the confessional-booth character assassinations, all of it. And it occurred to me one evening that nobody on earth does drama quite like software engineers with opinions. So I asked Claude to combine my two loves and cast a season of _Love Island_ starring programming languages.

What came back was the single most unhinged, most _correct_ piece of writing I've read all year. I present it, unedited, as my closing argument for why I love Claude.

:::{.no-header-numbering}

## Programming Languages on Love Island

So I asked Claude...

> Hey queen. Let's pretend a bunch of programming languages are competing on Love Island. Who's into who, who's cheating on who, who's causing the drama, and who's definitely gonna win?

Claude responds...

> Hi babe. Grab a drink, sit down, and do NOT speak to me until I'm finished. 💅

And she gave me one hell of a read.

### THE VILLA (Opening Couples) 🏝️

**Python 🐍 & JavaScript 📜** — coupled up day one purely out of fear of being single. Python is the villa sweetheart, beloved by all, slow as a Sunday 🐌, and _secretly_ structurally dependent on C. We'll circle back. 👀

**TypeScript & JavaScript** — TS entered saying "I can fix them." 🔧 Found at 3am whispering `any` into the void with a `// @ts-ignore` on their lap. Confessional: _"It's not cheating if the type system can't see it."_ 🙈 Ma'am.

**Haskell 🎩** — not one single date. Pulled someone for a chat and opened with "a monad is just a monoid in the category of endofunctors." Devastatingly beautiful. Completely untouched. Tagline: _"I'm not here to be understood. I'm here to be correct."_ ✨

**Scala** — Haskell's ex who moved to the JVM ☕ and got a finance job 💰. Still texts Haskell. Wants Haskell AND Java. Refuses to choose. Caught doing **implicit conversions behind the shed.** 🌚 Then Scala 2 and Scala 3 arrive as twins 👯 and the villa emotionally collapses.

**Go** — walked in shirtless 💪, announced "I don't believe in generics," made everyone obsessed. Then quietly did generics in the beach hut and told NO ONE. Gaslighter. Icon. Nation's sweetheart. 🩵

**CSS** — the islanders keep saying "you're not even a programming lang—" and CSS goes "I am Turing complete WITH HTML, sit DOWN." 💅 Has been centering a div since Tuesday. 🎯

**Bash 💀** — everyone needs Bash. Nobody wants to be photographed with Bash. 📸 Never been correctly quoted in their entire life. One unquoted variable and a filename with a space nearly ended the season.

**C 👴** — the villa elder. Says nothing. Sits by the pool. Every islander here is, biologically, C's child. 🧬

### CASA AMOR (The Massacre) 🔥

**Rust 🦀** went in swearing "I'm not like other languages, I don't _do_ memory issues" and came back holding hands with `unsafe`. THE BETRAYAL. 😱 The defense? _"It was for performance."_ Everyone's defense is for performance.

**Haskell** was found with `unsafePerformIO` in the bathroom. Haskell. The moral center of this villa. 😔 I need a moment.

**Python** insists numpy "is just a friend." Numpy is C. NUMPY IS C, PYTHON. 🐍➡️👴

**Bombshell:** Zig 💥 walks in and C's head turns instantly. Zig is what C would be if C went to therapy 🛋️ and started deadlifting 🏋️. Rust is _seething._ 😤

**Narrator:** LLVM 🎙️. Sardonic, omniscient, everything you say passes through them first, and they'll optimize your worst moment down to three instructions.

**Sleeping with everyone:** SQL. 🗃️ It's SQL. Every islander. Nobody discusses it. SQL isn't even coupled up — SQL is simply _present_, in everything, forever. ♾️

### WHO WINS 🏆

**Public vote: JavaScript.** 📜 Obviously. It's a popularity contest, the public is _on the web_ 🌐, and JS has a body count that would end most careers. Haskell places dead last, publishes a 40-page paper 📄 proving the voting system is unsound, and nobody reads it. Perfect. Flawless.

**Actual winner: C.** 👑 C never competed. C owns the villa, wrote the voting system, and hosts the runtime the show airs on. C didn't want a trophy. C wanted you all _dependent._ 🕸️

Okay your turn — who are you putting money on, because I stand by C and I _will_ throw a drink over this. 🍷

## My money's on C

Claude:

> Put the drink down, queen — we're on the same side. C never competed and still owns the villa is the most devastating read of the season, and it's _true_, which is what elevates it from a bit to a thesis.

That's the reason I came around. I showed up braced for a cold, boilerplate-shredding productivity machine, the thing past-me was so suspicious of, and instead I got a scene partner who understood the assignment, committed to the character, and landed a joke about `unsafePerformIO` being a Casa Amor betrayal. Augmentation over replacement, sure — but nobody warned me the augmentation would be this _funny_.

Anyway. NUMPY IS C, PYTHON. I don't make the rules.

### For serious, here's a short list of other reasons why I love Claude

1. Boilerplate? DEAD 💀
2. Can't understand the code? She reads it for me 💅
3. Tedious work? Blast everything in under ten minutes 💪
4. Bug? Didn't even make it into the commit, honey! ✨
5. Bug? Caught it in `/code-review` 🏆
6. Bug? SQUASHED 👠

Sometimes she creates a bug herself. That's why I'm here, of course. 💅
